# YouTube script — Prompting Is Not Architecture

**Runtime target:** 9 min

## Thesis

Viewers leave believing: **prompting optimizes moments; architecture and orchestration make operations survive**.

## Cold open (0:00–0:15)

| Say | Show |
|-----|------|
| Your prompt library is impressive. Your business still breaks when Sarah goes on vacation. That's not an AI problem. That's architecture. | Split screen: Notion prompt doc vs empty chair |
