# YouTube script — What Happens When a Business Has a Brain?

**Runtime target:** 10 min

## Thesis

Viewers believe: **a business brain is governed orchestration—AI that behaves through permissions and live context, not a chatbot guessing from documents.**

## Cold open (0:00–0:16)

| Say | Show |
|-----|------|
| Everyone wants a company brain. Most get a chatbot that guesses. There's a difference—and it shows up in behavior. | Chat UI giving a confident wrong answer vs grounded answer side-by-side (staged) |
